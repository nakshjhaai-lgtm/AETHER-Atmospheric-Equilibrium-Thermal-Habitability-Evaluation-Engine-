The Mathematical Core of AETHER

Client-side radiative transfer & similarity indexing.

01
Equilibrium Thermal Gradient

Teq = T★ · √( R★ / 2d ) · (1 − A)¼

Bond albedo-bound blackbody equilibrium at orbital distance d, before optical-depth correction.

02
Eddington Approximation — Surface Temperature

Ts4 = (¾) Teq4 · ( τs + ⅔ )

Plane-parallel grey atmosphere with Hopf function q(τ) ≈ ⅔. Enables runaway greenhouse transitions.

03
Bulk Density & Escape Velocity

ρ = Mp / (4/3 π Rp³)    ve = √(2GMp/Rp)

Derived from mass-radius inputs; core-material preset scales effective density.

04
Geometric-Mean Similarity Index

SI = ( SR · Sρ · Sv · ST )¼

Multi-class weighting against Earth, Mars and Venus baselines per Schulze-Makuch / Kopparapu conventions.

05
Kopparapu Effective Stellar Flux

Seff = Seff☉ + aT★ + bT★² + cT★³ + dT★⁴

4th-order polynomial boundaries (Recent Venus → Early Mars) evaluated per 1 M⊕ benchmark.

Citations Kopparapu et al. (2013) · Schulze-Makuch et al. (2011) · Kaltenegger & Sasselov
